About FoodBridge

A bridge between good food and real need.

Every day, edible food is thrown away while families nearby go hungry. FoodBridge connects donors with verified NGOs and food banks that can receive, store and redistribute safe food. Biogas partners provide a separate recovery route when food can no longer go to people.

Verified partners

Donors, NGOs, food banks and biogas partners are reviewed before their operational role is enabled.

Safety-first matching

Dietary needs, distance and safe-to-eat windows are checked together.

Dignity by design

Recipients find food discreetly, with clear access and collection details.

How It Works

From surplus to a shared meal — and safe recovery.

The network handles the coordination so donors can give quickly and community teams can focus on people, not paperwork.

01

Donors post surplus

Hotels, restaurants and kitchens add the food type, quantity, pickup window and safe-until time.

Takes under a minute
02

FoodBridge matches need

Our engine ranks nearby verified NGO and food-bank demand by food fit, urgency, capacity and route time. Unmatched edible food may be offered to a food bank; expired or unsafe food follows the biogas recovery path.

Safety & distance aware
03

Communities collect

The matched organisation confirms pickup, collects the food and records the handover for traceability.

Every handover counted

Get Involved

Four partners. One food rescue network.

FoodBridge connects food donors, NGOs and community organisations, food banks, and biogas facilities to move surplus safely and recover what cannot be redistributed.

Share surplus food

Turn excess into meals. Post a pickup, set a safe-until time, and let verified partners handle the rest.

Become a food donor For restaurants, hotels, caterers & kitchens

Bring food to your community

NGOs and food banks register needs and capacity. Food banks can also hold and redistribute in-date inventory.

Register your organisation Verification keeps the network safe for everyone

Operate a food bank

Receive suitable donor surplus, confirm delivery, manage inventory and make in-date stock available to nearby demand.

Join as a food bank Food banks are verified co-partners and can both receive and supply food
