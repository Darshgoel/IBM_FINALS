How matching works

A demand becomes an offer only when supply is safe and feasible

Safety and capacity first
Food type, dietary needs, preparation state, timing and your declared capacity are checked before a match.
You decide on offers
A pending offer is not food you have received. Accept only after confirming safety and collection feasibility.
Impact follows handover
Servings count only after the handover is confirmed, not when the match is created.
