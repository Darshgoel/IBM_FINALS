What happens next

A clear path from surplus to a safe handover

1. Feasibility matching
Nearby demand is checked for quantity, food type, receiving capacity, timing and distance.
2. Recipient decision
A recipient confirms food safety before accepting. Offers that are not accepted can be re-routed.
3. Collection & impact
Recovered food is counted only after the recipient confirms the handover.

Food safety reminder

Only post food that is fit for human consumption. When in doubt about temperature, time or handling, do not post it.

How these figures are calculated

Recovered food

Only listings with a completed handover are counted as recovered. Allocated or accepted food is still awaiting receipt confirmation.

Meals equivalent

Estimated using FoodBridge's standard conversion of approximately 3.5 meals per kilogram of prepared food. Different units are converted before totalling.

Waste avoided

The displayed CO₂e estimate uses a documented planning factor applied to recovered kilograms. It is an estimate, not a measured emission reduction.

Surplus predictions

Statistical forecasts built from your recorded surplus history. They are planning estimates, not guarantees.

Forecasts need history

Post at least two surplus records to generate a prediction. Accuracy appears after your first full forecast day closes.
