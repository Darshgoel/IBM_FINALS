Allocation order

How the matching service prioritises surplus before reserve routing

  1. 1
    Immediate demand

    Emergency and immediate registered needs that pass distance, capacity and safety checks.

  2. 2
    Regular demand

    Planned or recurring needs from verified demand organisations.

  3. 3
    Reserve point

    A food bank or storage hub with enough declared capacity for the batch.

  4. 4
    Recovery review

    No feasible target. The post remains available for later matching and is flagged for coordinator review.
