How we calculate

Transparent directory inputs, honest labels.

Each figure below maps to a field the network actually stores. Nothing is projected to look larger than the live directory supports.

Access points

Only active, verified food-serving organizations with a demand profile are returned by the public recipient directory. This includes food banks. Biogas facilities are not food collection points and are excluded from access counts.

Incoming matches

Counts pending and accepted matches assigned to a partner. These are allocations awaiting confirmed receipt, not completed handovers.

Capacity & areas

Meal capacity comes from food-serving partners' published serving capacity. Areas are distinct localities parsed from verified access-point addresses; biogas capacity is tracked separately as recovery intake.

These are access and capacity figures, not completed-handover totals. Do not read them as meals already rescued.
