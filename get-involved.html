What we ask of partners

Reliability matters more than volume.

Food rescue depends on trust. These commitments are what make a handover safe and worth repeating.

Describe food honestly

Quantity, preparation state, dietary tags and safe-until times should be accurate. The match depends on it.

Update the status

Confirm pickups, mark collections and complete handovers. Cancelled or expired food should not be left looking available.

Speak up early

If a pickup is at risk, say so. Coordinators can rebalance the network when there is still time to act.

Not sure which door to use?

Tell us what you have or what you need. We will point you to the right portal or person.
