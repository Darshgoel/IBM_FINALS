Before you write

Some questions already have a clear answer.

These are the questions the team receives most often. If your question is not here, use the form.

I have surplus food right now. What should I do?
Sign in to the donor portal and post the surplus with a realistic pickup window and safe-until time. If your organisation is not verified yet, create an account and a coordinator will review it. For an urgent large-volume rescue, call the coordination desk.
Can I choose a specific NGO to receive my food?
The standard network flow matches supply automatically based on dietary fit, urgency, capacity and distance. If you already work with a community partner, mention it in your message and the team can advise on the best route.
How do I register my NGO or community kitchen?
Use the NGO portal's registration form. You will need your organisation name, address, operating hours and approximate meal capacity. New accounts are reviewed before matching unlocks.
Do you publish donor names or recipient details?
No. Public pages show aggregate impact and open access-point information. They do not expose donor contact records, recipient identities, tokens or internal allocation data.
I found a problem with a listing or handover.
Use the form and choose “Technical or account issue”. Include the listing or organisation name if you can. Coordinators can review flags, disputes and compliance records in the admin portal.
